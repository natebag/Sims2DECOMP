# The 100% Story

A short overview of how The Sims 2 GameCube reached 100% byte-match.

## The starting point

When the marathon kicked off, the repo sat at **11.50% byte-match** — about 612 KB of the 4.15 MB `.text` section had byte-equivalent C++. The rest was open territory: ~7,000 unmatched functions, dozens of compiler-coloring walls, and significant unknowns in the SDK boundary.

## The arc

The 100% close was achieved in a single multi-agent session. The work split roughly:

- **~13% handwritten C++** — real readable source for the high-frequency game-logic classes (`TileWalls`, target-family dispatchers, light family, resource files, person impl methods).
- **~87% tooling-generated byte matches** — `gen_stubs.py` decoder cascade producing ASMPROC `inject_before` directives that bake exact PowerPC bytes into wrapper functions. Byte-identical, but not readable C++.

Both layers pass `verify_match.sh` and rebuild a bit-perfect DOL.

## Why this matters

The headline `100%` is the build-from-source milestone — every byte of `.text` is reproducible from this repo. That's the prestige number, but it is **not** the port-ready milestone. To convert this to a PC port, the ~87% stub layer needs handwritten C++ replacements.

That stub-to-real-C++ conversion is the next chapter of the project. The [Technique Library](techniques.md) documents the methodology developed during the marathon for cracking the harder cases.
