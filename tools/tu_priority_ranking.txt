## Notes on Estimation Methodology

- **<50B**: Likely leaf functions (no bl instructions) - easiest to match
- **50-100B**: Simple stwu+bl patterns - moderate difficulty
- **100-200B**: Multiple calls or loops - harder but possible
- **200B+**: Complex logic - unlikely to match without source
- **Est. Potential**: Sum of <50B + 50-100B, realistic 50-70% success rate
